DotX Representative is a brand and startup consultancy grounded in direct commercial experience across the sports sector. The practice works with early stage founders on advisory, brand identity, web design and build, photography and brand events, with a particular focus on golf and sports businesses.

The team behind DotX Representative has first-hand experience of taking a sports brand from concept to a commercially trading business. That experience informs every engagement.

Matthew Lynas (Founder and Director) leads strategy, commercial operations and client development. Craig Leith (Co-Founder and Lead Consultant) leads creative, design and digital delivery. The practice operates under Acqua Utilities Media Limited.
