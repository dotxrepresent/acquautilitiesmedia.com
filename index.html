About

IT Consultancy
with experience.

Acqua Utilities Media Limited is a UK based IT consultancy specialising in energy (Oil and Gas, Renewables) and utilities (electricity and water). The company provides advisory and contracting services across infrastructure delivery, lifecycle operations and business development.

The team brings together senior IT programme management, Microsoft Power Platform and process automation, data analytics and corporate web design and build. Through DotX Representative, Acqua also provides brand strategy and startup consultancy with a focus on the sports sector.

Every engagement runs under a clear scope of work, with changes controlled and signed off by the client. Acqua works directly with clients and stakeholders to deliver practical, accountable outcomes.
