About

IT Consultancy
with experience.

Acqua Utilities Media Limited is a UK based IT consultancy specialising in energy (oil and gas, renewables) and utilities (electricity and water). The company provides advisory and contracting services across infrastructure delivery, lifecycle operations and business development.

The team brings together senior IT programme management, corporate web design and build, Microsoft Power Platform and process automation, and data analytics. Web and automation work is delivered by dot.x, Acqua's digital studio run by Matthew Lynas and Craig Leith, which also brings brand and sport sector experience.

Every engagement runs under a clear scope of work, with changes controlled and signed off by the client. Acqua works directly with clients and stakeholders to deliver practical, accountable outcomes.

Privacy Policy

Who we are

Acqua Utilities Media Limited is registered in Scotland, United Kingdom. Contact: acquautilities@sthelier.onmicrosoft.com

What we collect

When you submit an enquiry we collect your name, email address, company name and message. We do not collect payment information.

How we use it

Your information is used solely to respond to your enquiry. We will never sell or share your personal data with any third party for marketing purposes.

Storage

Submissions are processed via Formspree (GDPR compliant) and delivered to our team inbox, after a Google reCAPTCHA check to block spam. No data is stored on this website.

Cookies

We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising cookies. Our enquiry forms use Google reCAPTCHA to protect against spam. reCAPTCHA sets cookies and collects technical information such as your IP address that are strictly necessary for this security check, and its use is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. When you dismiss our cookie notice, that choice is saved in your browser so the notice is not shown again.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete any data we hold. Contact acquautilities@sthelier.onmicrosoft.com to exercise these rights.
