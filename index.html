About

IT Consultancy
with experience.

Acqua Utilities Media Limited is a UK based IT consultancy specialising in energy (Oil and Gas, Renewables) and utilities (electricity and water). The company provides advisory and contracting services across infrastructure delivery, lifecycle operations and business development.

The team brings together senior IT programme management, data analytics, and commercial business development capability. Through dot.x Representative, Acqua also provides brand strategy, web design and startup consultancy with a focus on the sports sector.

Acqua works collectively with clients and stakeholders to deliver the best possible outcomes. We believe that with the right leadership and the right tools, there is always a better way to deliver.
