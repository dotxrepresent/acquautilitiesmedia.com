About Acqua Utilities Media

IT consultancy, built on experience.

Acqua Utilities Media Limited is a UK based IT consultancy specialising in energy (oil, gas, renewables) and utilities (electricity, water). Acqua supports infrastructure delivery, lifecycle operations and business development, on an advisory or contract basis.

The team brings together senior IT programme management, corporate web development, Microsoft Power Platform automation and data analytics. Web and automation work is delivered by DotX Representative, Acqua's digital studio.

Every engagement runs under a clear scope of work, with changes controlled and signed off by the client. Acqua works directly with clients and stakeholders to deliver practical, accountable outcomes.

How we work

Scope agreed · A written scope of work before anything starts.

Change control · Any change is agreed with the client first.

Sign off · Outcomes reviewed and signed off by the client.
