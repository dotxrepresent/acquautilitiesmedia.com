The opportunity

Paying for capability you are not using.

A typical Microsoft 365 subscription includes tools to build apps, automate workflows and report on live data. Yet in most businesses, work still moves through emailed spreadsheets, paper forms and people chasing approvals.

That costs time, accuracy and visibility, and the fix is often already licensed. We find where the effort is going and put the tools you own to work.

Does this sound familiar?
  • Key processes live in spreadsheets that get emailed around
  • Approvals and sign offs happen by chasing people over email
  • Staff re-key the same data into more than one system
  • Monthly reports are built by hand, and out of date on arrival
  • Paper or PDF forms are still used on site and in the field
  • You pay for Microsoft 365 but only really use Outlook and Excel
What we build

The full Power Platform, joined up.

Each tool is useful on its own. Real value comes from connecting them, with data captured once in an app, moved by an automated flow and reported live in Power BI.
