Recent work

Web Development · 2026 Energy Sector Client
Corporate website rebuild for an oilfield engineering products manufacturer

A long established UK manufacturer of oilfield engineering products needed its corporate website modernised. The old site had inconsistent styling, dated content, weak mobile performance and gaps in compliance.

Acqua was engaged under a formal engagement letter with a defined scope of work. The WordPress site is being rebuilt in Bricks Builder on a dedicated test environment, so the live website stays untouched until launch. We work directly with the Managing Director and the client's technical lead, and every change request is tracked and signed off.
